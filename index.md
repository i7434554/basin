# Basin Support

Basin turns your voice memos into text on your iPhone. If something doesn't work as expected, the answers below may help. For anything else, write to i7434554@gmail.com — please include your iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of what happened.

## Getting started

- Tap the coral record button (on every screen) to start a memo. You can also start one from a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget, Control Center, the Action button, or by asking Siri to "Start a memo in Basin".
- Tap stop when you're done. The memo opens with its transcript.
- On Apple Watch, open Basin (or tap its complication) and it starts recording. Tap stop, and the memo is sent to your iPhone, where it is transcribed.

## Frequently asked questions

**The record screen says the microphone is off.**
Basin needs microphone access to record. Open the Settings app › Basin and turn on Microphone. If another app or a call is using the microphone, finish that first and try again.

**My words don't turn into text right away.**
The first time you record in a language, iOS downloads Apple's speech recognition model for it. Recordings made in the meantime are kept and transcribed automatically once the model has arrived.

**How do I change the transcription language?**
In Basin, open Settings › Language and pick a language. Basin uses your iPhone's language by default.

**A recording from my Apple Watch hasn't appeared on my iPhone.**
The watch sends each recording to your iPhone when the two are connected. Keep the iPhone nearby with Bluetooth on and open Basin on both; waiting recordings are sent automatically.

**Where is the summary button?**
Summaries use Apple Intelligence. On devices that support it, open a memo, tap the More (…) button and choose the sparkles icon. On other devices the button is hidden.

**I deleted a memo by mistake.**
Deleted memos stay in Settings › Recently Deleted for 30 days. Open it, select the memo and restore it.

**Where are my memos stored?**
Only on this iPhone, in Basin's own storage — Basin has no account or server. Like other app data, they are included in your iPhone's own backups (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) when backups are turned on. To keep a copy somewhere else, share a memo as text, an image card or a Markdown note.

## Privacy

Basin collects no data. Recording, transcription and summaries all happen on your device. See the [privacy policy](https://i7434554.github.io/basin/privacy.html) for details.
